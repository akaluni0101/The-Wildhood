Editorial Story

THE WILDHOOD JOURNEY

Where architectural luxury converges with the untamed spirit of nature.

01 / Architecture & Design

THE LIVING SPACE

Nestled on a sprawling estate in Sector 100, Sukhgarh, The Wildhood breaks away from sterile urban restaurant molds. Our design balances dramatic architectural lines with lush botanical growth.

Large dark-framed glasshouse pavilions invite the natural canopy indoors. Solid aged-timber tables, stone masonry paths, and pampas grass landscaping form an organic retreat where every seat enjoys panoramic views of the open greens and animal grounds.

Real Glasshouse Pavilion & Stone Path
02 / Changing Lights

THE ATMOSPHERE

The Wildhood experiences two distinct, magical personalities each day. In the daytime, gentle sunlight filters through the tree leaves, creating a breezy sanctuary perfect for quiet artisanal coffees, book reading, and watching resident animals graze.

As twilight embraces Mohali, the venue undergoes a mesmerizing metamorphosis. Warm amber lanterns ignite across the decks, fairy lights shimmer through the trees, and the air cools into an enchanted nocturnal forest that hums with warmth until 1:00 AM.

Visitor Tip:

We recommend arriving between 4:30 PM and 5:30 PM to experience the golden hour with the animals, and staying to witness the starlit illumination.

03 / Resident Companions

THE WILDLIFE SOUL

Few places in the country allow you to dine in the calm presence of graceful horses, miniature ponies, and friendly animals under open skies. The Wildhood was conceived as a celebration of coexistence.

Our resident animals reside in spacious, ethically maintained paddocks and open green lawns. Watching their gentle movements right alongside your table brings an unhurried, grounded peace that city life rarely affords.

04 / Culinary Craft

THE WILDHOOD TABLE

Great atmosphere demands extraordinary food. Our culinary philosophy centers on time-honored artisanal techniques: slow-fermented pizza dough baked at 450°C, handmade pasta ribbons with shaved forest truffles, and garden-infused smoked elixirs.

Whether indulging in a comforting Continental main or a vibrant fusion appetizer, every plate is prepared with honest ingredients, uncompromised craft, and deep respect for flavor.

05 / Unforgettable Gatherings

THE MOMENTS WE CREATE

The Wildhood is a canvas for your most cherished memories. From romantic anniversary dinners under the boughs to exuberant family reunions where children delight in feeding carrots to our friendly ponies.

We also cater to curated private gatherings, photography sessions, and celebratory milestones with tailored culinary arrangements and bespoke decor setups.
