A Sanctuary in Sukhgarh

NOT JUST A CAFÉ.
A WILD EXPERIENCE.

Tucked away in Sector 100, Mohali, The Wildhood redefines hospitality. Here, the boundaries between a lush nature sanctuary and upscale gastronomy dissolve. Breathe in the crisp evening air, listen to the gentle rustle of leaves, and watch gentle horses graze as your wood-fired pizza emerges golden from the stone oven.

Botanical Glasshouse

Floor-to-ceiling glass pavilions enveloped in living greenery, dark slate, and warm amber pendant lights.

Golden Hour Paddock

Open-air garden decks facing the animal enclosures. Arrive before sunset for unforgettable moments.

Clay & Wood Fire

Authentic sourdough pizzas, slow-braised ragus, and aromatic Continental delicacies crafted to perfection.

Starlit Dining

As night falls, fairy lights and warm lanterns transform the grounds into an enchanted forest until 1:00 AM.

WHEN THE FOREST
COMES ALIVE

As dusk settles over Sukhgarh, The Wildhood transforms. Hundreds of warm amber string lights flicker awake, illuminating ancient boughs. Fire pits crackle softly under starry skies while ambient acoustic tones fill the night air.
