MEET THE WILD

Get to know our resident animal family and the wildlife spirit woven into every corner of the café.

Real Resident Companions

THE ROYAL HORSES & PONIES

The crown jewels of our nature estate. Our gentle groomed horses and adorable miniature ponies graze freely on the open garden lawn right alongside guest dining tables, bordered by palm trees and tranquil green canopies.

Accustomed to humans, they welcome calm greetings and gentle pats. Guests cherish capturing golden hour photographs with them while enjoying coffee on the adjacent outdoor lawn.

★ Best Visiting Time: 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM (Before Twilight)
Aquatic Sanctuary

THE POND DUCKS & WATERFOWL

Our crystal stone pond is home to a cheerful flock of white ducks and waterfowl. Surrounded by natural river stones, blooming water lilies, and emerald ferns, this peaceful water body is the tranquil heartbeat of the estate.

Watching them glide effortlessly across the water while hearing gentle splashing creates an unmatched meditative dining ambiance.

★ Feature Area: Central Water Garden
Sheltered Nook

WOODLAND BUNNY HAVEN

Nestled under shady foliage and rustic hollow logs, our playful domestic rabbits hop happily through soft moss and natural clover patches.

Designed with great care for their safety and comfort, this enclosure is an absolute delight for young children and families visiting during the day.

★ Family Highlight: Kid-Friendly & Calm Space
Real Wildhood Installations

SCULPTURAL GUARDIANS & ART

In addition to our live resident companions, The Wildhood interior features magnificent artistic installations—including carved elephant heads, deer mount sculptures on rustic brickwork, and majestic wildlife statues set among living indoor plants.

Under the warm glow of branch chandeliers and Edison bulbs, these artistic centerpieces celebrate the untamed beauty of wildlife.

★ Interior Highlight: Authentic Brickwork & Glasshouse Art
Compassionate Coexistence

OUR ETHICAL ANIMAL WELFARE CHARTER

At The Wildhood, animal health, dignity, and wellbeing always come first. Our resident companions are not mere attractions—they are cherished members of our estate family.

VETERINARY SUPERVISION

Our horses, waterfowl, and rabbits receive regular health inspections, customized organic nutrition, and dedicated round-the-clock caretakers.

NATURAL SPACIOUS HABITATS

Clean natural grazing paddocks, open-sky freshwater ponds, and shaded resting enclosures designed to preserve their natural behavioral habits.

GENTLE GUEST ETIQUETTE

We enforce strict guidelines: no sudden loud noises, no unauthorized external feeding, and gentle, supervised petting only.
